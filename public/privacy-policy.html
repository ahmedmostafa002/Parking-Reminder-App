Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Summary (Google Play Data Safety Pledge):

SpotSaver is engineered with a privacy-first approach. Your precise parking coordinates, garage photos, floor notes, and parking meter timers are stored exclusively on your device's local memory and are never uploaded, synchronized to any private cloud server, or sold to third parties.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the SpotSaver: Parking Reminder App ("SpotSaver", "we", "us", or "our"), available on the Google Play Store and web browsers. We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. If you have questions regarding this policy, please contact our support team at Myworkemail2023@gmail.com.

2. Information We Access and How It Is Used

A. Precise and Approximate Location Data (GPS)

  • Purpose: When you tap "Park Here" or use the Find My Car walking directions, SpotSaver accesses your device's precise location (GPS latitude and longitude) to record your parked vehicle's position and compute distance and bearing directions back to your car.
  • Storage: All coordinates are recorded directly into your device's local browser / app storage (LocalStorage / IndexedDB). Location data is never transmitted to our servers or sold.
  • Permission: Location access is requested via Google Play's standard runtime permission dialog (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) accompanied by an explicit in-app prominent disclosure.

B. Camera and Photos Access (Optional)

  • Purpose: SpotSaver allows you to snap a photo of your parking stall, floor level, pillar number, or garage section.
  • Storage: Photos are saved entirely on your device's local storage and are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • Permission: Camera access (CAMERA) is requested only when you actively trigger the "Add Photo" or "Take Photo" feature.

C. Device Notifications (Optional)

  • Purpose: SpotSaver utilizes local device notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) to alert you before your parking meter timer expires.
  • Operation: Meter countdown alerts are scheduled and executed locally on your device without relying on external push servers.

D. Bluetooth and Audio Disconnect (Smart Detection - Optional)

  • Purpose: To offer automatic spot saving when your device disconnects from your vehicle's Bluetooth audio system or Android Auto.
  • Operation: Processed entirely on-device to trigger local auto-save.

3. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and maintenance, the free version of SpotSaver integrates Google AdMob to display non-intrusive banner advertisements strictly on the Parking History screen (never over active map controls or navigation).

  • AdMob Data Collection: Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID), device IP address, operating system version, and ad interaction metrics to serve relevant or non-personalized advertisements, detect fraud, and report performance.
  • AdMob Privacy Policy: For details on how Google processes ad data, please review the Google Ads & Privacy Policy.
  • Opt-Out & Ad-Free Option: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings (Settings > Google > Ads). You can also permanently remove all advertisements via the one-time SpotSaver Pro unlock.

4. Google Play Data Safety Compliance

  • Data Collection: Precise Location (Functional, stored on-device), Photos (Functional, stored on-device), Device Identifiers / Advertising ID (Processed by Google AdMob).
  • Data Sharing: We do not share personal user data with any third party, except as handled by Google AdMob for advertising fulfillment.
  • Data Security: All spot logs and garage notes are isolated within the app's protected sandbox environment.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: You have 100% control over your data. Tapping "Clear All" on the History screen or selecting "Delete Save" permanently purges all saved locations and photos from your device instantly.

5. GDPR and EU/EEA/UK User Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland:

  • SpotSaver displays a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) / consent banner before serving personalized advertisements.
  • You have the right to withdraw consent, access your stored data, and request complete data deletion at any time.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Disclosures

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Under the CCPA, California residents have the right to request information regarding data categories collected, request immediate deletion of personal data, and opt out of any personalized advertising.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SpotSaver is a general utility app designed for motor vehicle drivers aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
